How to Use the Bible & Audio Bible

This page explains the reader as it works today. Reading is free. You do not need an account.

Finding books and chapters

On the home page, tap Start reading for the first available book, Continue if you left off mid-chapter, or All books. Books in the top menu opens the same list. Tap a book, then a chapter number. The home page also shows how much of the Bible is translated so far.

While you are reading, the book and chapter name at the top of the screen can be used to move around, and the buttons at the bottom of the chapter go to the previous or next chapter.

The top menu

The bar at the top of every page has:

  • CTB — back to the home page.
  • Read, Devotional, Downloads, How to use, and Books.
  • A books icon and a search icon.
  • A menu (three sliders) for reading settings, bookmarks, and highlights.
  • Language — described below.

Reading settings

Open the menu to change:

  • Text size — A− and A+.
  • Appearance — Auto (follows your phone), Light, or Dark.
  • Footnotes — show or hide the translators’ notes in the chapter.
  • Open where I left off — when this is on, the reader can return you to the last verse you were in.

The same menu has links to Bookmarks, Highlights, Backup, and Account.

Search

Tap the search icon and type a word or phrase. Results come from the translated English text. Turn on Include footnotes if you also want matches in the notes.

Bookmarks, highlights, and footnotes

Tap a verse. A toolbar appears:

  • The bookmark control saves or removes that verse.
  • Yellow, green, blue, or pink highlights the verse. The eraser removes a highlight.
  • Listen from this verse starts the audio Bible there.

Open your saved list from the menu, or from Bookmarks and highlights on the home page. Backup saves a file of your bookmarks, highlights, and place on this device, and can restore one. That file is your notes, not a copy of the Bible text.

Small letter marks in the text are footnotes. Tap one to read it in a panel, or scroll to Footnotes under the chapter.

Listening to the audio Bible

Tap a verse, then Listen from this verse. A player opens at the bottom with previous verse, play or pause, next verse, and stop. The verse being read is marked and is scrolled into view as you listen.

The last button on the player opens Speed and voice:

  • Speed — from 0.6× to 2× (1× is normal).
  • Narration — when a recording exists for the chapter, you can choose a recorded female or male voice, or a dramatized reading (cast, music, and sounds) with a female or male narrator. If a dramatized chapter is not recorded yet, the regular recorded voice is used. If there is no recording, your device voice reads it.
  • Voice — picks the device voice when a recording is not playing. Automatic is the default. On an iPhone, you can download a Premium or Enhanced English voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices, then choose it here.
  • Keep going into the next chapter — continues automatically.
  • Sleep timer — off, a set number of minutes, end of chapter, or a custom time. Audio fades out and pauses when the timer ends. On an iPhone a web page cannot fade the volume, so it simply pauses.

Language

The Language menu can show a machine translation of the page through Google. That translation may contain errors. The English text is the official Clay Jar Treasure Bible. Nothing is sent for translation until you pick a language other than English. A notice appears when a machine translation is on.

Optional sign-in

You can read without an account. Sign in only if you want your place in the text, bookmarks, highlights, listening place, and listening settings (such as speed and voice) saved to your account so another device can use them. Signing in is free. Use Google, GitHub, or email from the home page or from Account in the menu. Text size and light or dark stay on this device. Signing out asks whether to keep or remove the copy on this device. Your account stays until you delete it. The Clay Jar Translation can use the same sign-in and keeps its own progress.

Add to your Home Screen

iPhone or iPad: open the site in Safari (not an in-app browser). Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open the site in Chrome. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

The icon then opens like an app. There is no App Store or Play Store fee for this. The Downloads page has the same iPhone steps.

Offline reading and updates

Once you have opened the site (and a book) while online, that copy can be read without a connection. When you are online again, the app checks for a new version. To see an update right away, close the Home Screen app (swipe it away) and open it again. If it still looks old, do that once more.

Downloads

This Bible is free to read online. A full file of the whole Bible (PDF, EPUB, or similar) is not offered, because the text is copyrighted. The Downloads page explains how to keep the reader on your phone and how you may quote brief passages with credit. For anything more, write to the contact address below.

Daily devotional email

On the home page or the Devotional page you can subscribe to the day’s reading, sent at 4:00 in the morning in your time zone. It is free. You confirm once by email, and you can unsubscribe any time. After you subscribe, check your spam or junk folder for the confirmation message, and consider adding contact.clayjar@protonmail.com to your contacts. Email delivery is not guaranteed.
