paras: 1 5 10 14 23
head 1: The End Has Come
head 5: Disaster Is Coming
head 10: The Day of Panic
head 14: No One Will Fight
head 23: Rulers Will Fail
1 A message from Yahweh came to me.
2 "Mortal one, this is what the Lord Yahweh says about the land of Israel.{a} The end! The end has come to all four corners of the land.
3 Now the end is on you. I will let My anger loose against you. I will judge you by the way you lived, and I will pay you back for all your hateful acts.
4 I will show no pity. I will charge you for how you lived, and your hateful acts will stay with you. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.
5 This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Disaster after disaster is on its way!
6 The end is here. The end is awake and rising against you. It is coming!
7 Your turn has come,{b} you who live in this land. The time is here. The day is close. On the mountains there will be panic, not happy shouts.
8 Soon I will pour out My rage on you and use up My anger against you. I will judge you by your ways and charge you for every hateful act.
9 I will show no pity. I will charge you for how you lived, and your hateful acts will stay with you. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, am the one who delivers the blow.
10 The day is here! It is coming! Your turn has come. Pride has grown into a rod, and it has flowered.
11 Violence has grown into a rod that punishes evil. Nothing will be left of the crowd, their wealth, or anything of value among them.{c}
12 The time has come. The day is here. The buyer should not cheer, and the seller should not cry, for anger burns against the whole crowd.
13 A seller will never return to what he sold, as long as both men live. The vision covers the whole crowd, and it will not be undone. Because of their sins, none will hold on to life.{d}
14 The trumpet has sounded and all is ready. Still, nobody goes out to fight, since My anger is on that whole crowd.
15 Swords wait outside. Disease and hunger wait inside. Whoever is in the fields will be killed by a sword. Whoever is in the city will be swallowed by hunger and disease.
16 Some will escape to the mountains, moaning like doves of the valleys, each one grieving over his own sin.
17 Every hand will go limp. Every knee will turn to water.{e}
18 They will put on sackcloth and shiver in terror. Shame will cover every face, and every head will be bald.
19 They will toss their silver on the streets, and gold will become garbage to them. On the day of Yahweh's anger, neither can save them. Neither can satisfy their hunger or fill their stomachs, because they caused the fall into sin.
20 Their lovely jewelry made them proud. They shaped their hateful idols and ugly statues out of it. So I will turn it into garbage for them.
21 Foreigners will carry it off as loot. The earth's wicked people will win it as a prize. They will make it unclean.
22 I will look away while they ruin My treasured place. Thieves will walk in and make it unclean.
23 Make chains,{f} because murder fills the land and violence fills the city.
24 I will bring the cruelest of the nations to take their homes. The pride of the strong will end, and their holy places will be ruined.
25 Terror will arrive! They will search for peace and find nothing.
26 Trouble will follow trouble, and rumor will follow rumor. They will beg a prophet for a vision. But the priest will have no teaching left, and the elders will have no advice.
27 The king will mourn, and the prince will dress in despair. The common people will tremble. I will treat them as their ways deserve, and I will judge them by their own judgments. That is when they will learn that I am Yahweh."
=a The Greek puts verses 3–9 in a different order and in a shorter form.
=b The Hebrew word is tsephirah, a word of uncertain meaning. It is also in verse 10. Others translate it "doom" or "crown."
=c The Hebrew of verse 11 is hard to understand.
=d The Hebrew of verse 13 is hard. "Keep his life" may also mean "make himself strong."
=e That is, they will wet themselves from fear.
=f The meaning of this phrase is uncertain, and the Greek leaves it out.
