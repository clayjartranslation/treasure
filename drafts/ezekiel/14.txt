paras: 1 12
head 1: Idols in the Heart
head 12: Noah, Daniel, and Job
1 Some of Israel's elders came and sat down in front of me.
2 Yahweh spoke to me again.
3 "Mortal one, these men have let idols settle in their hearts. They keep their sin right in front of them, ready to trip them. Why should I let them ask Me anything?
4 So speak to them. Tell them, 'The Lord Yahweh says: Any Israelite who holds idols in his heart and keeps his sin before his eyes, and then comes to a prophet, will get his answer from Me. I, Yahweh, will answer him myself, and the answer will fit his pile of idols.
5 I will do this to win back the hearts of Israel's people, for every one of them has drifted away from Me through his idols.'
6 "So tell the people of Israel, 'The Lord Yahweh says: Turn back! Leave your idols, and turn from every one of your vile habits.
7 Any Israelite, or any foreigner living with Israel, who pulls away from Me and holds idols in his heart, and who keeps his sin before his eyes and then comes to a prophet to ask Me a question, will get his answer from Me. I, Yahweh, will answer him Myself.
8 I will turn against that man. I will make him a warning that people repeat as a saying. I will remove him from My people, and you will know that I am Yahweh.
9 "'And if a prophet is fooled into giving an answer, it is I, Yahweh, who allowed that prophet to be fooled. I will raise My hand against him and destroy him from among My people Israel.
10 Both will carry their guilt. The prophet and the man who asked him will be equally guilty.
11 Then Israel will stop wandering away from Me. They will stop making themselves unclean with their sins. They will be My people, and I will be their God, says the Lord Yahweh.'"
12 Another message from Yahweh came to me.
13 "Mortal one, suppose a country sins against Me by turning from Me. I reach out My hand against it. I cut off its food supply and send hunger, so that people and animals die.
14 Even if Noah, Daniel,{a} and Job lived there, their goodness would save only themselves, says the Lord Yahweh.
15 "Or suppose wild animals roam that country. They leave it without children, and the land becomes so empty that nobody dares cross it, for fear of the beasts.
16 I swear by My own life, says the Lord Yahweh, that those three men could not save even their own sons or daughters if they were there. Only they would be rescued, and the land would be a wasteland.
17 "Or suppose war comes to that country at My command. 'Let swords sweep across it!' I say, and people and animals are cut down.
18 I swear by My own life, says the Lord Yahweh, that those three men could not rescue sons or daughters there. Only they would be rescued.
19 "Or suppose I send a deadly sickness into that country and pour out My anger in blood, so that people and animals die.
20 As surely as I live, says the Lord Yahweh, Noah, Daniel, and Job could not save a son or a daughter. Their goodness would save only themselves."
21 This is what the Lord Yahweh says: It will be far worse when I send all four of My terrible punishments against Jerusalem: war, hunger, wild animals, and sickness. They will destroy people and animals alike.
22 Yet some survivors will be left there, sons and daughters who are led out. They will come to you, and you will see how they have lived and what they have done. Then you will be comforted about the disaster I brought on Jerusalem, about everything I did to it.
23 You will find comfort when you see their lives and actions. You will know that I did nothing to Jerusalem without a reason, says the Lord Yahweh.
=a The Hebrew spelling is Danel (as also in 28:3). He was a righteous hero of ancient times, known from texts found at Ugarit. Jewish tradition connects him with the Daniel of the book of Daniel. The name means "God is my judge."
