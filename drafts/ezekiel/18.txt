head 30: Turn Back and Live
1 Another message from Yahweh came to me.
2 "Why do you keep repeating this old line about the land of Israel: / 'Parents eat grapes that are still green, / but it is the children's teeth that tingle'?
3 I swear by My own life, says the Lord Yahweh, you will stop using that saying in Israel.
4 Listen: every life belongs to Me. The father's life is Mine, and the child's life is Mine too. Death comes to the person who sins, not to someone else.
5 "Picture a man who does what is right. He treats people fairly and lives the right way.
6 Hill shrine meals are not for him. He will not lift his eyes toward the idols of Israel's family. Another man's wife is not someone he touches, and he keeps his distance from a woman during her monthly bleeding.
7 He cheats nobody. If he took a person's coat as a promise to pay a debt, he gives it back. He does not steal. Hungry people receive food from him, and people with no clothes receive something to wear.
8 Interest is not what he charges when he lends, and he takes no extra fee. He refuses to do wrong, and when two people argue, he judges them fairly.
9 He follows My rules and carefully keeps My decisions. A man like that is in the right. He will live, says the Lord Yahweh.
10 "Now imagine that this same man fathers a son, a robber, a man who spills blood, and who does any of the things on this list—
11 things his father never did. This son eats at the hill shrines and sleeps with another man's wife.
12 He crushes the poor and the needy. He steals. He keeps the coat that was left as a promise. He looks up to idols and does things I hate.
13 He lends for interest and charges a fee on top. Can a son like that live? He cannot. All these disgusting acts are his, and death will be his end. The blame for that death rests on him alone.
14 "But suppose that wicked man also has a son. This son sees every sin his father committed. He sees them and refuses to copy them.
15 Meals at the hill shrines are not for him. Israel's family idols do not catch his eye. Another man's wife is not someone he touches.
16 He cheats nobody. He does not keep a coat taken as a promise, and he does not steal. Hungry people receive food from him, and he covers anyone who lacks clothing.
17 The poor he refuses to harm.{a} He takes no interest and no extra fee. He follows My decisions and walks in My rules. His father's guilt will not cause his death. He will live.
18 As for his father—that man squeezed people for money, robbed a fellow Israelite, and did what was evil among his own people. He will die for his own guilt.
19 "Still you ask, 'Why does the son not pay for what his father did?' If that son has practiced fairness and honesty, kept all My rules, and lived by them, life will be his.
20 It is the sinner who must die. A child will not bear a father's guilt, and a father will not bear a child's guilt. Each good person keeps the credit of his own right living, and each wicked person keeps the blame of his own evil.
21 "But if a wicked person leaves behind every sin of his past, keeps all My rules, and begins to live with fairness and honesty, life will be his. Death will not be.
22 None of the wrongs he once committed will be held against him. Because of the right things he now does, he will live.
23 Do I enjoy it when a wicked person dies? asks the Lord Yahweh. Would I not far rather see him turn from his path and live?
24 "But if a person who has been doing right walks away from it and starts doing wrong—the same hateful acts that wicked people do—can he live? The good he once did will not be added up in his favor. The betrayal he committed and the sins he did will be why he dies.
25 "Yet you say, 'The Lord's path does not measure up.' Listen, family of Israel: Is My path the crooked one? Or do your own paths fall short?
26 When a person who has been doing right leaves that path and does wrong, he will die for it. The wrong he has done is why he dies.
27 And when a wicked person walks away from the evil he once did and starts living with fairness and honesty, that turn will keep him alive.
28 He took a hard look at his life, left behind every sin he had done, and so life will be his. Death will not be.
29 Still Israel's family says, 'The Lord's path is not just.' Family of Israel, is the problem in My path? The unfairness sits in your paths, not Mine.
30 "So I will judge you, family of Israel, each of you by how you have lived, says the Lord Yahweh. Turn around. Leave all the sins you have done, so that guilt will not bring you down.
31 Cast aside every rebellious act that has been yours, and let your heart be made new, and your spirit too. Family of Israel, why should you die?
32 I take no pleasure in anyone's death, says the Lord Yahweh. So turn around, and live!"
=a The main Hebrew text says "from the poor." The Greek says "from injustice." Compare verse 8.
