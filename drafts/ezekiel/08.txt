paras: 1 5 7 14 16
head 1: A Vision of the Temple
head 5: The Idol at the Gate
head 7: Pictures on the Wall
head 14: Women Weeping for Tammuz
head 16: Men Bowing to the Sun
1 On the fifth day of the sixth month (in August–September),{a} in the sixth year, the Judean leaders came to my house and sat down near me. There the hand of the Lord Yahweh landed on me.
2 I looked and saw a figure like a man made of fire.{b} Below His waist He looked like fire. Above His waist He glowed like shining metal.
3 He stretched out what seemed to be a hand and took hold of my hair. The Spirit lifted me up high between earth and sky. In visions from God, He carried me to Jerusalem. He set me at the entrance of the north gate of the inner courtyard, where a jealousy-making idol stood.
4 There too was the glory of Israel's God, shining as it had in the vision from the valley.
5 God told me, "Mortal one, look north." I looked, and there at the entrance north of the altar gate stood the idol that makes God jealous.
6 He asked, "Mortal one, do you see what they are doing? Israel does terrible, hateful things here that drive Me from My holy place. You will see even worse."
7 He led me on to the courtyard gate, where I noticed a hole in the wall.
8 "Mortal one," He said, "dig through the wall." I dug, and I found a doorway.
9 He said, "Go in and look at the wicked, hateful things they are doing in there."
10 Inside, I saw pictures cut into every wall: crawling creatures, unclean animals, and all the idols of Israel.
11 Seventy of Israel's elders stood in front of the pictures. Jaazaniah,{c} son of Shaphan,{d} stood among them. Each man held a pan for burning incense, and a cloud of sweet smoke rose up.
12 God asked me, "Mortal one, do you see what Israel's elders do in the dark? Each man does it in his own room full of carved idols. They say, 'Yahweh cannot see us. Yahweh has left the land.'"
13 He added, "You will see them do even worse things."
14 Next He brought me to the north entrance of Yahweh's house. Women were sitting there, crying over the god Tammuz.{e}
15 He said, "Mortal one, did you see that? You will see worse things than these."
16 Then He took me into the inner courtyard of Yahweh's house. Between the porch and the altar, at the door of Yahweh's temple, stood about twenty-five men. Their backs were to the temple of Yahweh, and they faced east. They were bowing to the rising sun.
17 He said, "Mortal one, do you see this? Is it too small a thing for Judah to do these hateful acts here? They also fill the land with violence and keep making Me angry. They even hold a branch to their noses!{f}
18 So I will answer in fury. I will not pity them or spare them. They may shout in My ears, but I will not listen."
=a The Hebrew says "in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month." Months are counted from spring.
=b The Hebrew says "fire." The Greek says "a man," which many scholars prefer. Compare 1:27.
=c Jaazaniah means "Yahweh listens."
=d Shaphan means "rock badger." He was probably the king's secretary in the time of Josiah.
=e Tammuz was a god of Mesopotamia. People mourned his death each summer.
=f No one is sure what this means. It may describe a ritual gesture for a false god, or an insult.
