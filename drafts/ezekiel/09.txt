paras: 1 3
head 1: Six Men with Weapons
head 3: A Mark on the Faithful
1 God shouted so I could hear: "Bring the city's punishers here, each with his weapon of ruin in hand!"
2 Six men arrived from the direction of the upper north gate. Each one carried a smashing weapon. With them came one more man, dressed in linen, with a writer's kit hanging at his side. All of them walked in and halted next to the bronze altar.
3 Up from the winged guardian{a} where it had rested rose the glory of Israel's God, and it moved to the temple's threshold. Yahweh then called to the man in linen with the writer's kit.
4 Yahweh told him, "Go through Jerusalem, all of it. Draw a mark{b} on the forehead of each person who sighs and cries over the hateful things done there."
5 Then I heard Him tell the other men, "Follow him through the city and strike. Do not pity anyone. Do not hold back.
6 Kill old men, young men, girls, children, and women, until they are all gone. Anyone wearing the mark, you must not touch. My own holy place is where you start." The elders in front of the temple were the first to fall.
7 He told them, "Make the temple unclean. Fill its courtyards with the dead. Go!" They went out and struck down people all over the city.
8 While they were killing, I was left alone. I threw myself face down and shouted, "Oh no, Lord Yahweh! As You pour out Your anger on Jerusalem, will You wipe out all who remain in Israel?"
9 He replied, "The sin of Israel and Judah is huge. Murder fills the land, and unfairness fills the city. They say, 'Yahweh has left the land. Yahweh does not see.'
10 So I will give them no pity and no mercy. I will make them pay for what they have done."
11 The man in linen, the writer's kit at his side, then returned with his report: "I did exactly what You told me."
=a Cherubim were winged beings that guarded holy places. See Genesis 3:24. These are probably the cherubim over the ark in the Most Holy Place.
=b The Hebrew word is taw, the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet. In the writing of that time it looked like an X or a cross.
