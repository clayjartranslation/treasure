paras: 1 17
head 1: False Prophets
head 17: False Prophetesses
1 A message from Yahweh came to me.
2 "Mortal one, speak against the prophets of Israel who make up messages from their own minds. Tell them to hear the word of Yahweh.
3 This is what the Lord Yahweh says: How terrible for foolish prophets! They follow their own spirit and have seen nothing.
4 Israel, your prophets are like wild dogs in a pile of ruins.
5 You never went up to the cracks in the wall. You never fixed the wall for Israel, so it could stand in the fight on the day of Yahweh.
6 What they see is false, and their fortune-telling is a lie. They say, 'Yahweh says,' when Yahweh did not send them. Still they wait for their words to come true.
7 You saw a false vision and told a lying fortune. You said, 'Yahweh says,' but I never spoke.
8 So the Lord Yahweh says: You have spoken lies and seen false things. Now I am against you, says the Lord Yahweh.
9 My hand will come down on prophets whose visions are false and whose fortunes are lies. They will have no seat in My people's council. No record of Israel will carry their names. Their feet will never enter Israel's land. Then you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.
10 They have pulled My people off the path. They cry, 'Peace,' when there is no peace. Someone builds a flimsy wall, and they paint it with whitewash.
11 Tell those whitewashers that the wall will fall. A flooding rain will come. Great hailstones will drop, and a stormy wind will break loose.
12 When the wall falls, people will ask you, 'Where is the whitewash you put on it?'
13 So the Lord Yahweh says: In My anger I will send a storm wind. In My rage there will be flooding rain and hailstones that destroy.
14 I will knock down the wall you covered with whitewash. I will flatten it, and its base will show. When it falls, you will die under it. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.
15 I will finish My anger on the wall and on those who whitewashed it. Then I will say to you, 'The wall is gone, and so are those who covered it.
16 These were the prophets of Israel who spoke to Jerusalem. They saw visions of peace for her, though no peace existed,' says the Lord Yahweh."
17 "Mortal one, now speak against the women among your people who prophesy from their own minds.
18 Tell them what the Lord Yahweh says: Shame on you women who tie magic bands{a} around every wrist and make veils to fit heads of all sizes, so you can hunt souls! Do you think you can hunt down the lives of My people and still keep yours safe?
19 For a few handfuls of barley and some crumbs of bread, you have brought shame on Me among My people. By your lies you have put to death some who should not die, and spared some who should not live. My people were happy to hear lies.
20 So the Lord Yahweh says: I will fight your magic bands, the traps you use to catch people like birds. I will rip them from your arms and let your captives fly free.
21 Your veils I will tear away, and out of your grasp I will pull My people. They will never be your prey again. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.
22 With lies you made honest people sad, though I had not hurt them. You cheered up the wicked, so they never turned from evil to save themselves.
23 That is why your false visions and fortune-telling will end. I will pull My people free from your grip, and you will know that I am Yahweh."
=a No one is sure what the Hebrew words for "bands" and "veils" mean. They were probably objects used for magic.
