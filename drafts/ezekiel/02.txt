paras: 1 8
head 1: Sent to a Stubborn People
head 8: The Scroll
1 God spoke to me: "Mortal one,{a} get up on your feet. I have words for you."
2 While He was still speaking, the Spirit came into me. I was set upright, and I listened closely.
3 "Mortal one," He said, "Israel is where I am sending you. That people has turned on Me. Their fathers fought against Me, and so do their children, right up to this day.
4 Their faces are hard, and their hearts will not bend. You are going to them with this message: 'The Lord Yahweh says so.'
5 Maybe they will listen, and maybe not, for they rebel against Me. Either way they will learn that a prophet has been among them.
6 Do not fear them, mortal one. Do not fear their talk, even if you seem to be sitting in thorn bushes or on scorpions. Their words need not frighten you, and their glares need not shake you. They rebel against Me.
7 Speak My words to them, listening or not. They are rebels.
8 As for you, mortal one, hear what I say. Do not rebel like those rebels. Open your mouth and eat the food I hand you."
9 Then I saw a hand reaching toward me. Inside it lay a scroll.
10 He unrolled it before my eyes. Both sides were covered in writing. It was all sad songs, weeping, and warnings of disaster.
=a The Hebrew is ben adam, "son of a human being." It means "human one" or "mortal." It reminds Ezekiel that he is only a man, and God is God. God calls him this about ninety times in this book.
