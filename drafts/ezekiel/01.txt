paras: 1 4 15 22 26
head 1: Visions by the Chebar Canal
head 4: Four Living Creatures
head 15: The Wheels
head 22: The Throne above the Sky
1 On the fifth day of the fourth month (in June–July),{a} in the thirtieth year,{b} I sat among the exiles near the Chebar canal.{c} Heaven split open, and God showed me visions.
2 The fifth day of that month fell in the fifth year since King Jehoiachin had been taken away as a captive.{d}
3 There in Chaldean country, by the Chebar canal, Yahweh spoke to the priest Ezekiel,{e} the son of Buzi.{f} The power of Yahweh came on him there.
4 A storm wind swept down from the north. I saw a giant cloud flashing with fire, ringed by bright light. Deep inside the fire, something glowed like shining metal.{g}
5 Out of the fire came four creatures that were alive. They looked something like people,
6 Each of them owned four faces, and each owned four wings.
7 The legs were straight. The feet looked like calf hooves, and they sparkled like polished bronze.
8 Under the wings, on each of the four sides, were hands like human hands. All four creatures had faces and wings,
9 Wing touched wing from one creature to the next. As they traveled, they never turned. Each simply moved forward.
10 Look at the four faces. A human face looked out the front. A lion's face was on the right. An ox's face was on the left. An eagle's face was at the back.
11 That described their faces. Above, each creature stretched out two wings to meet the wings of its neighbors. It used its other pair to cover its own body.
12 Wherever the spirit{h} led, the creatures followed, straight ahead, with no turning.
13 Among the creatures moved what seemed to be burning coals or flaming torches. They drifted back and forth. The fire glowed bright, and bolts of lightning shot out of it.
14 The creatures themselves raced back and forth, as fast as lightning flashes.{i}
15 While I was looking at the creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground beside each of the four of them.
16 The wheels were made like this. They sparkled like a gem of golden-green color.{j} All four looked the same. Each wheel seemed to have a second wheel crossing through its middle.
17 They could roll in any of the four directions without turning around.
18 The rims were very high, and they were frightening. All around, the rims of the four wheels were full of eyes.
19 As the creatures traveled, the wheels traveled beside them. As the creatures lifted off the ground, the wheels lifted too.
20 Wherever the spirit led, they went, and the wheels were lifted up beside them, for the creature's spirit lived in the wheels.{k}
21 The wheels copied the creatures. If the creatures went, the wheels went. If the creatures paused, the wheels paused. If the creatures rose, the wheels rose, since the creature's spirit lived in the wheels.
22 Above the creatures' heads was something like a dome. It glittered like ice and was awesome to see.{l} It spread out over their heads.
23 Under the dome, the creatures held out their wings straight, so that each touched the next. Each creature also used two wings to cover its body.
24 When they moved, I could hear their wings. The sound was like rushing floodwater, like the voice of Shaddai,{m} or like the noise of a great army. When they stopped, they folded their wings.
25 Then a voice sounded from above the dome over their heads, while they stood with their wings folded.
26 High over the dome there was a throne that looked like deep blue stone.{n} Seated on it was a shape like a man.
27 Above His waist, He gleamed like shining metal, as if flames were wrapped around Him. Below His waist, He looked like fire. A bright glow surrounded Him.
28 Think of a rainbow after rain, shining in the clouds. That is how bright the glow around Him was. This was the glory of Yahweh as I saw it. I dropped to the ground on my face, and then I heard a voice speak.
=a The Hebrew says "in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month." Months here are counted from spring.
=b It is not clear what year this counts. It may be Ezekiel's age, since priests began their work at thirty. It may be some other starting point.
=c The Hebrew word can also mean "river." The Chebar was a canal near Nippur in Babylonia.
=d This was in 593 BC. Jehoiachin was taken to Babylon in 597 BC.
=e Ezekiel means "God makes strong" or "may God make strong."
=f Buzi may mean "my scorn." The meaning is not certain.
=g The Hebrew word is hashmal, a rare word of uncertain meaning. The Greek has "amber." The same word is in verse 27.
=h The word can also mean "wind." This is also true in verse 20.
=i Verse 14 is not found in the Greek (Septuagint).
=j The Hebrew word is tarshish, a gem of uncertain kind, maybe beryl or topaz.
=k The Hebrew has "the spirit of the living creature." The word "creature" is singular here.
=l The Hebrew word for "dome" can also mean "platform" or "expanse." It is the same word as in Genesis 1:6.
=m Shaddai is a name for God. It is often translated "the Almighty." Its first meaning is uncertain.
=n The Hebrew word is sappir, probably lapis lazuli, a deep blue stone.
