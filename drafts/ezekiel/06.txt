paras: 1 8 11
head 1: Against the Mountains
head 8: A Few Will Escape
head 11: Death Everywhere
1 A message from Yahweh came to me.
2 "Mortal one, face the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them.
3 Tell them: Mountains of Israel, hear what the Lord Yahweh says! He calls to the mountains and hills, to the creek beds and valleys. 'I am bringing a sword against you. I will tear down your hilltop shrines.{a}
4 Your altars will be wrecked, and your incense stands will be smashed. Your dead will fall right before your idols.{b}
5 I will place Israel's dead bodies before their idols. Around your altars I will spread their bones.
6 Wherever you live, towns will become ruins. Hilltop shrines will be torn down. Altars will be wrecked and left empty. Idols will be broken to pieces. Incense altars will be cut down, and all that your hands made will be wiped out.
7 Dead bodies will lie among you, and you will know that I am Yahweh.
8 'Still, I will keep some of you alive. A few will get away from the sword and live among the nations, scattered across many lands.
9 Those who get away will think of Me while they are prisoners among the nations. They will remember how their unfaithful hearts broke My heart, and how their eyes chased after idols. They will be disgusted with themselves over the evil and hateful things they did.
10 They will learn that I am Yahweh. My threat to bring this disaster on them was not empty.'
11 "This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Clap your hands and stomp your foot. Cry out, 'How awful!' Look at all the hateful things Israel has done! They will fall by the sword, by hunger, and by disease.
12 Those far away will die of disease. Those nearby will fall to the sword. Those who survive that will die of hunger. In this way I will pour out all My anger on them.
13 You will know that I am Yahweh when their dead lie among the idols and around the altars. Bodies will lie on each high hill and mountaintop, under every green tree and thick oak, in the very places where they burned sweet incense to their idols.
14 My hand will stretch out against them. Their land will become a bare wasteland, from the desert to Diblah,{c} in every place they live. That is when they will learn that I am Yahweh."
=a Hilltop shrines were places for worship, often of other gods.
=b The Hebrew word for idols is gillulim, a word of contempt. It may mean "dung-gods." It is used about forty times in Ezekiel.
=c No one knows where Diblah was. Many scholars read Riblah, a town in Syria, because the Hebrew letters for d and r look alike.
