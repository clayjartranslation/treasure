paras: 1 6
head 1: The Useless Vine
head 6: Fuel for the Fire
1 Yahweh spoke to me again.
2 "Mortal one, is a grapevine's wood worth more than the wood of any other tree in the forest?
3 Can anyone make something useful from it? Could you carve even a peg from it to hang a tool on?
4 No. People toss it on the fire as fuel. The flames burn both ends, and the middle turns black. Is it good for anything then?
5 It was useless even when whole. Once flames have eaten it and left it scorched, surely nothing can be made from it!
6 So the Lord Yahweh says: I gave the vine's wood to the fire as fuel. It was only one kind of tree among all the trees of the forest. I will do the same to the people of Jerusalem.
7 I will turn against them. They got out of one fire, but another fire will burn them up. Then, when My anger falls on them, you will know that I am Yahweh.
8 I will make the land empty, because the people turned away from Me, says the Lord Yahweh."
