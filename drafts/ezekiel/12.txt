paras: 1 7 8 17 21 26
head 1: Packing for Exile
head 7: Ezekiel Does What God Said
head 8: What the Sign Means
head 17: Eating in Fear
head 21: No More Delays
1 A message from Yahweh came to me.
2 "Mortal one, you live among rebels. Their eyes work, yet nothing registers. Their ears work, yet nothing sinks in. That is because they are rebels.
3 So prepare a bag as if you were being sent into exile. In broad daylight, with the people watching, leave your home for another place. They may catch on, although they are rebels.
4 In the daytime, carry out your bag in front of them, like an exile's bag. Then in the evening, go out yourself in front of them, like someone being taken away.
5 With everyone looking on, break a hole in the wall and pass your things through it.
6 As they watch, hoist the bag to your shoulder and leave in the dark. Cover your face to keep the land out of sight. You are My warning sign to the house of Israel."
7 I did exactly as I was told. In the daytime I carried my bag out, like an exile's bag. In the evening I dug through the wall with my hands. In the dark I lifted the bag onto my shoulder and carried it out while they watched.
8 The next morning, a message from Yahweh came to me.
9 "Mortal one, did the rebel people of Israel ask you, 'What are you doing?'
10 Tell them what the Lord Yahweh says: This message{a} concerns the prince in Jerusalem and every Israelite living in that city.
11 Tell them, 'I am a sign for you. Whatever I did, they will go through. Prisoners, hauled off to exile.'
12 The prince among them will shoulder his bag at dusk and slip out. A gap will be broken in the wall for his escape. His own face will be covered, so the land stays hidden from his eyes.
13 My net will fall over him, and My trap will close. I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans. His eyes will never see that land, and there he will die.{b}
14 All who surround him, his helpers and his armies, I will scatter to every wind. I will chase them with a drawn sword.
15 They will know that I am Yahweh once I spread them among the nations and sow them across the lands.
16 Yet a few I will keep alive through sword, hunger, and disease. Wherever they end up among the nations, they will confess all the hateful things they did. That is when they will know that I am Yahweh."
17 A message from Yahweh came to me.
18 "Mortal one, eat your bread while you shake. Drink your water while you tremble and worry.
19 Then tell the people of the land that the Lord Yahweh says this about those living in Jerusalem, in Israel's land: They will eat bread in fear and drink water in terror. Everything in their land will be taken away, since all its people have been so violent.
20 Towns where people live will be ruined, and the land will become a desert. Then you will know that I am Yahweh."
21 A message from Yahweh came to me.
22 "Mortal one, what is this proverb you repeat in Israel's land? You say, 'Time drags on, and no vision comes true.'
23 Tell them that the Lord Yahweh says: I will stop that proverb. Nobody in Israel will repeat it again. Say this instead: 'The days are close. Every vision is about to happen.
24 There will be no more false visions, and no more smooth lies from fortune-tellers in Israel.
25 I am Yahweh. When I speak, My word will happen, and it will not be put off. In your own days, you rebels, I will speak and I will do it, says the Lord Yahweh.'"
26 A message from Yahweh came to me.
27 "Mortal one, the people of Israel are saying, 'The vision he sees is for a time far off. He prophesies about the distant future.'
28 So tell them that the Lord Yahweh says: No more waiting for any of My words. Whatever I say will happen, says the Lord Yahweh."
=a The Hebrew word is massa, which can mean "burden" or "message." It plays on the bag that was carried, and on the word for prince, nasi, which means "one lifted up."
=b King Zedekiah was blinded before he was taken to Babylon. See 2 Kings 25:7.
