paras: 1 5 13
head 1: Hair Cut and Divided
head 5: Why Jerusalem Is Punished
head 13: Anger Spent
1 "Mortal one, pick up a sharp sword and treat it as a barber's razor. Run it across your head and chin. Then weigh the cut hair on scales and split it into three piles.
2 When the days of the siege end, set one pile on fire inside the city. Chop the second pile with the sword around the city's edge. Toss the last pile to the wind, and a drawn sword of Mine will go after it.
3 But pick out a few hairs and tie them in the edge of your robe.
4 Take a few of those again and throw them into the fire. A fire will spread from there to all of Israel."
5 This is what the Lord Yahweh says: "I put Jerusalem in the middle of the nations, with lands all around her.
6 She has turned My laws into wicked acts, worse than the nations did. She has done the same with My rules, worse than the lands around her. She has thrown out My laws and has not lived by My rules.
7 So the Lord Yahweh says: Your trouble-making beats that of every nation near you. You ignored My rules and disobeyed My laws. You did not even match what the nations around you consider right.{a}
8 So the Lord Yahweh says: I Myself have turned against you. While the nations look on, I will punish you.
9 Because of all your hateful acts, I will do to you what I have never done and will never do again.
10 In your city, mothers and fathers will eat their sons and daughters, and grown children will eat their parents. I will carry out My punishments on you, and I will blow every survivor in all directions.
11 As surely as I live, says the Lord Yahweh, you filled My holy place with ugly idols and hateful acts, and so I will turn away.{b} I will not pity you or spare you.
12 One third of you will die of sickness or hunger inside your city. One third will fall by the sword outside it. I will scatter the last third to every wind and chase them with a drawn sword.
13 Then My anger will be finished. My fury against them will be satisfied, and I will be calm. When I have poured it all out, they will know that I, Yahweh, spoke out of My deep love for My own name.
14 I will make you a ruin and a joke to the nations around you, in the sight of everyone who walks by.
15 You will be shamed and laughed at. You will be a warning and a shock to the nations around you, when I punish you in anger and rage. I, Yahweh, have spoken.
16 I will shoot the arrows of hunger at you. They are arrows that destroy, and I will send them to destroy you. I will make the hunger worse and cut off your bread.
17 Hunger and wild animals will come from Me, and they will take your children. Disease and bloodshed will sweep past, and a sword from Me will fall on you. I, Yahweh, have spoken."
=a Some ancient copies leave out the word "not" here. They say, "but have lived by the standards of the nations around you."
=b Or "I will cut you down." The Hebrew verb is uncertain.
