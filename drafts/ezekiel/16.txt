head 15: The Unfaithful City
head 23: Idols on Every Street
head 30: Paying Her Own Lovers
head 35: Punishment for Jerusalem
head 44: Worse Than Her Sisters
head 53: A Promise of Return
1 Yahweh spoke to me again.
2 "Mortal one, make Jerusalem face her disgusting deeds.
3 Tell her, 'The Lord Yahweh says to Jerusalem: Canaan is where you began and where you were born. A man from the Amorites fathered you, and a Hittite woman gave you birth.{a}
4 When your birth day came, no one cut the cord, washed you, rubbed you with salt, or wrapped you in cloths.
5 Nobody felt sorry enough to do any of this for you. You were unwanted, so someone threw you out into an open field right after you were born.
6 "'I walked past and saw you kicking in your blood. Lying there in that blood, you heard Me command, "Live!" Yes, "Live!" was My word to you in that bloody place.{b}
7 I caused you to grow as a field plant grows. You became tall and very lovely.{c} You reached young womanhood, yet you were completely bare.
8 "'Later I walked by again and saw that the time for love had come for you. I laid the edge of My robe over you and covered your body. I made a solemn promise and entered a covenant with you, says the Lord Yahweh, and you became Mine.
9 I washed the blood from you with water and rubbed oil on your skin.
10 I dressed you in colorful cloth and gave you sandals of fine leather.{d} I wrapped you in linen and covered you with silk.
11 Then I gave you jewelry: bracelets for your wrists and a necklace.
12 A jewel hung from your nose, rings from your ears, and a lovely crown rested on your head.
13 You wore gold and silver. Your clothes were linen, silk, and colorful cloth. Your food was the finest flour, along with honey and olive oil. You became very beautiful, and you ruled as a queen.
14 The nations heard of you because of your beauty. It was flawless, for I had put My own glory on you, declares the Lord Yahweh.
15 "'Yet you trusted in your own beauty. You used your fame to give yourself to other gods. You offered yourself to every person who walked by, and your beauty belonged to him.{e}
16 You took some of your clothes and made bright little shrines. There you gave yourself to other gods. Such things should never happen.
17 You took the beautiful jewelry that I had given you, made of My gold and silver. You made statues of men from it and gave yourself to them.
18 You put your colorful clothes on them. My oil and My incense were what you laid before them.
19 I had given you food: the best flour, olive oil, and honey. You put it before those statues as a sweet smell. That is what you did, says the Lord Yahweh.
20 "'You took the sons and daughters you had borne for Me and killed them as food for those statues. Was your unfaithfulness not bad enough already?
21 You killed My children and handed them over, making them pass through fire for those statues.
22 In all your disgusting deeds and unfaithfulness, you never remembered your young days, when you lay bare and kicking in your blood.
23 "'After all this evil you did (how sad for you, says the Lord Yahweh!), you set up a platform and a high place in every public square.
24 You raised up your own mound there. Every public square had a high place of yours.
25 At the head of every street you built a high place. You made your beauty something disgusting. You offered yourself to everyone who passed by, again and again.
26 You gave yourself to the Egyptians, your neighbors with strong desires.{f} You did it again and again to make Me angry.
27 So I reached out My hand against you and made your land smaller. I gave you up to the Philistine women, who hate you. Even they were shocked at your shameful ways.
28 You were never satisfied, so you gave yourself to the Assyrians too. Yet even with them you were left wanting more.
29 You went on to give yourself to Babylonia, the land of traders.{g} Even then you were not satisfied.
30 "'Your heart is so weak and burning,{h} says the Lord Yahweh, when you do all these things like a bold and shameless woman.
31 At the head of every street you built your platform, and in every square you made your high place. But you were not like other such women, because you refused to be paid.
32 You are a wife who cheats! You welcome strangers in place of your husband.
33 Other women receive gifts, but you were the one who gave gifts to every lover. You paid them to come to you from all around.
34 You are the opposite of other women. Nobody chased you. You paid, and nobody paid you. You were the opposite.
35 "'So hear the word of Yahweh, unfaithful city.
36 The Lord Yahweh says: You poured out your desire,{i} and you showed your bare body to your lovers. You did it with your idols, and you gave them the blood of your children.
37 So now I will call together all your lovers, the ones you enjoyed, whether you loved them or hated them. They will come from every direction to stand against you. I will take off your clothes before them, and they will look at you, completely bare.
38 I will judge you as the law judges women who cheat on their husbands or shed blood. I will bring on you the anger and jealous fury of blood.
39 I will hand you over to them. They will pull down your platform and smash your high places. They will strip off your clothes and take your fine jewelry, leaving you with nothing on.
40 A mob will come against you. Its people will stone you and hack you to pieces with swords.
41 They will burn down your houses and punish you while many women watch. I will stop your unfaithfulness, and you will never pay lovers again.
42 Then My anger against you will cool. My jealousy will leave you. I will be calm and no longer angry.
43 You did not remember your young days. You made Me furious with all this. So now I will make you pay for what you did, says the Lord Yahweh. Did you not add this shameful act to all your other disgusting deeds?
44 "'Look, everyone who loves sayings will say about you: "Like mother, like daughter."
45 You are truly your mother's daughter, for she hated her husband and her children. Your sisters hated their husbands and children too, and you are like them. Your mother came from the Hittites, and your father from the Amorites.
46 Your older sister is Samaria. She and her daughters lived on your north side.{j} Your younger sister is Sodom. She and her daughters lived on your south side.
47 It was not enough for you to walk in their ways and copy their disgusting deeds. Very soon you were acting even worse than they did.
48 As surely as I live, says the Lord Yahweh, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did anything like what you and your daughters have done.
49 Here is the guilt of your sister Sodom: She and her daughters were proud. Food was plentiful and life was easy, but they never helped the poor and needy.
50 They became arrogant and did disgusting things before Me. So I took them away, as you have seen.{k}
51 Samaria's sins were not even half of yours. You have done more disgusting things than she did. Next to all your deeds, your sisters look good.
52 Now you must carry your shame, for you spoke up for your sisters. Your sins were so much worse than theirs that they look better than you. So be ashamed and carry your shame, for you made your sisters look good.
53 "'Yet I will bring them back from captivity: Sodom with her daughters, Samaria with her daughters, and you with them.
54 Then you will carry your shame and be embarrassed by all you did. In this you will comfort them.
55 Sodom and Samaria, with their daughters, will be restored to what they once were. Your own turn will come as well, you and your daughters.
56 In your proud days you would not even speak your sister Sodom's name.
57 That was before your evil was uncovered. Now you are like her. The daughters of Edom{l} and all the people around her look down on you, as do the Philistine women and everyone around you.
58 You must carry the weight of your shameful deeds and disgusting acts, says Yahweh.
59 "'The Lord Yahweh says: I will repay you for your own actions. You looked down on your promise and broke the covenant.
60 Still, I will remember the covenant I made in your early days. I will set up a covenant with you that lasts forever, and then you will remember your ways.
61 Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your sisters, both older and younger. I will give them to you as daughters, though not as part of your covenant.
62 I Myself will set up My covenant with you, and you will know that I am Yahweh.
63 You will remember and be ashamed. You will be too embarrassed to open your mouth, when I forgive you for everything you did, says the Lord Yahweh.'"
=a The Amorites and Hittites were among the peoples living in Canaan before Israel. The point is that Jerusalem's beginnings were not Israelite.
=b The Greek does not have the second "Live!"
=c The Hebrew is uncertain here. It may mean "you came to have the finest jewels."
=d The Hebrew word is tahash, a fine leather. We do not know what animal it came from.
=e The Hebrew words "your beauty belonged to him" are uncertain.
=f Hebrew: "great of flesh," a plain way of speaking of strong desire.
=g Hebrew: "the land of Canaan, Chaldea." Here "Canaan" means "traders."
=h Or "weak." The Hebrew word is uncertain.
=i The Hebrew word is nechosheth, which may mean "copper" (that is, money) or "desire." It is uncertain.
=j Hebrew: "on your left." Facing east, the left side is north.
=k Or "as you saw."
=l Some ancient copies say "Edom." The Hebrew text and the Greek say "Aram" (Syria). The two names differ by one similar letter.
