paras: 1 5 13 14 22
head 1: Evil Leaders in the City
head 5: Judgment on the Leaders
head 13: A Promise for the Exiles
head 22: The Glory Leaves Jerusalem
1 The Spirit lifted me and set me down at the east gate of Yahweh's house. Twenty-five men stood at its entrance. Among them I saw two leaders of the people: Jaazaniah son of Azzur{a} and Pelatiah{b} son of Benaiah.{c}
2 God told me, "Mortal one, these are the men who make wicked plans and give harmful advice in this city.
3 They say, 'Building homes will happen soon. Think of this city as a kettle and ourselves as the choice meat inside.'{d}
4 So speak against them. Prophesy, mortal one."
5 Then the Spirit of Yahweh came on me, and He said, "Give them this message from Yahweh. People of Israel, I know what you have said and what goes on in your minds.
6 Many have died by your hands in this city. Its streets are piled with your victims.
7 So the Lord Yahweh says this: The dead you piled up inside the city are the meat, and the city is the pot. But I am going to drag you out of it.
8 You were afraid of the sword, so I will bring the sword against you, says the Lord Yahweh.
9 Out of the city I will haul you, and strangers will get you. I will carry out My punishments on you.
10 You will fall by the sword. At the edge of Israel's land I will pass sentence on you. Then you will learn that I am Yahweh.
11 No, this city will not be your kettle, and you will not be the meat in it. Out at Israel's border I will pass sentence on you.
12 You will learn that I am Yahweh. You did not follow My rules or keep My laws. You copied the nations around you instead."{e}
13 While I was still speaking God's message, Pelatiah son of Benaiah dropped dead. I fell on my face and cried out loudly, "Oh no, Lord Yahweh! Are You going to finish off everyone left in Israel?"
14 A message from Yahweh came to me.
15 "Mortal one, think of your brothers, your relatives,{f} and all the exiles of Israel. The people of Jerusalem say about them, 'They are far from Yahweh. This land was given to us to own.'
16 So give them this answer from the Lord Yahweh: It is true that I sent them far away among the nations and scattered them through the lands. Yet I have been a little holy place{g} for them in the lands where they went.
17 Give them this answer too from the Lord Yahweh: From the nations I will round you up. From the lands where you were scattered I will bring you home. The land of Israel will be yours.
18 When they return, they will clear out every ugly idol and hateful thing.
19 I will give them one heart{h} and a new spirit within. The heart of stone I will take away, and a soft, living heart will take its place.
20 After that, My rules will guide their steps, and they will respect My laws. They will be My people, and I will be their God.
21 But some people long for ugly idols and hateful things. I will make them pay for what they do, says the Lord Yahweh."
22 Then the cherubim opened their wings, with the wheels at their sides. The glory of Israel's God rose above them.
23 Yahweh's glory went up and away from the city, and rested on the mountain to its east.{i}
24 The Spirit lifted me up in the vision, by the Spirit of God, and took me to the exiles in Chaldea. Then the vision faded.
25 I told the exiles everything Yahweh had shown me.
=a Azzur means "helper." This Jaazaniah is not the man in 8:11.
=b Pelatiah means "Yahweh rescues." This gives meaning to the cry in verse 13.
=c Benaiah means "Yahweh has built."
=d The Hebrew can also mean, "It is not time to build houses." The pot picture shows that the leaders thought they were the best meat, safe inside the city.
=e Verses 11–12 are not found in the Greek.
=f The Hebrew says "the men of your redemption." These were relatives with the duty to buy back family land. See Leviticus 25:25. The Greek has "the men of your captivity."
=g Or "a holy place for a little while."
=h Some Hebrew copies and the Greek have "another heart." Others read "a heart not divided."
=i This is the Mount of Olives.
