paras: 1 4 9
head 1: A Model of the City
head 4: Lying on His Side
head 9: Rationed Food
1 "Mortal one, get a clay brick and lay it in front of you. Sketch Jerusalem on it.
2 Then show the city under attack. Build a wall of siege towers and a ramp against it. Place army camps around it, and set battering rams on every side.
3 Next, find an iron cooking sheet and stand it between yourself and the city, like a wall of iron. Aim your face at the city. It is surrounded, and you are the one surrounding it. All this is a sign for Israel.
4 Now lie down on your left side. You will carry the guilt of Israel. You will carry it for as many days as you lie there.
5 I am giving you one day for each year of their guilt. That is 390 days.{a} In this way you will carry the guilt of Israel.
6 After you finish, roll over and lie on your right side. For forty days you will carry Judah's guilt, one day standing for one year.
7 Turn your face toward Jerusalem under attack. Bare your arm, and speak against the city as a prophet.
8 I am putting ropes on you. You cannot turn from one side to the other until your days of attack are over.
9 Now take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt. Pour them into one jar, and make bread from them. You will eat this bread for all 390 days that you lie on your side.
10 Your food will be weighed. Eat about eight ounces{b} each day, at set times.
11 Water will be measured too: about two-thirds of a quart{c} a day, sipped at set times.
12 Bake your bread like a barley cake, and bake it in front of everyone over a fire of human waste."
13 Yahweh said, "This is how Israel will eat their bread: unclean, among the nations where I will drive them."
14 But I said, "Oh no, Lord Yahweh! I have never made myself unclean. From my youth until now, I have not eaten any animal that died on its own or was killed by wild beasts. Rotten meat has never entered my mouth."
15 "Fine," He said. "You may bake over cow dung and skip the human waste."
16 He went on, "Mortal one, I will soon break Jerusalem's supply of bread. They will weigh their bread and eat it in worry. They will measure their water and drink it in fear.
17 Bread and water will run short. People will stare at each other in terror, and they will waste away because of their guilt."
=a The Greek has "190 days" here and in verse 9, and "150 days" in verse 4. The Hebrew has 390.
=b The Hebrew says "twenty shekels." That is about 230 grams.
=c The Hebrew says "a sixth of a hin." That is about 0.6 liter.
