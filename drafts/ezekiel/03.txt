paras: 1 4 10 12 16 22
head 1: Eating the Scroll
head 4: A Hard Forehead
head 10: Ezekiel Goes to the Exiles
head 12: A Watchman for Israel
head 22: Ezekiel Cannot Speak
1 God said to me, "Son of man, eat what you have been given. Eat this scroll. Then go and speak to Israel."
2 I opened wide, and He put the scroll in my mouth.
3 "Mortal one," He said, "let this scroll I am handing over feed your stomach until you are full." So I swallowed it, and honey could not have tasted sweeter.
4 He said, "Son of man, go to Israel now. Tell them My words.
5 I am not sending you to people with a strange speech or a language that is hard to learn. I am sending you to Israel.
6 You are not going to many nations whose words you could not follow. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you.
7 Israel, however, will not listen to you, because Israel will not listen to Me. Every one of them is stubborn and hard-hearted.
8 So I will make you just as tough as they are. Your face will be as hard as theirs.
9 I will make your forehead like a diamond,{a} harder than flint rock. Do not fear them. Do not let their stares scare you. They are a rebel people."
10 He went on, "Son of man, keep in your heart every word I tell you. Listen well.
11 Go to the exiles, your own people, and give them the message: 'The Lord Yahweh says so.' Do this whether they listen or refuse."
12 Then the Spirit lifted me. Behind me came a loud rumbling: "Yahweh's glory is to be praised in the place where He lives!"{b}
13 It was the sound of the creatures' wings brushing against each other. It was also the wheels beside them, turning with a loud roar.
14 The Spirit raised me and took me away. I traveled with a bitter heart and a burning spirit, while Yahweh's hand pressed down hard on me.
15 At Tel-abib{c} I found the exiles who lived beside the Chebar canal. Seven days went by while I sat among them, stunned.
16 When the seven days ended, a message from Yahweh came to me.
17 "Mortal one, I am putting you on watch over Israel. Whenever you hear a word from My mouth, pass it on to them as My warning.
18 Say I tell a wicked man, 'You are going to die.' If you stay quiet and do not warn him to leave his evil road, he will die in his sin. His blood, though, will be on your hands.
19 But if you warn him and he still will not leave his evil road, he will die in his sin. Your own life, however, will be safe.
20 Or say a good man turns from what is right and does evil. I may set a stumbling block in front of him, and he will die. If you gave no warning, he dies in his sin, and none of his earlier good deeds will count. His blood will be on your hands.
21 If instead you warn that good man to stop sinning, and he stops, he will live, because he listened. You will have rescued yourself as well."
22 Yahweh's hand rested on me there. "Get up," He told me, "and walk out to the valley.{d} I will talk with you in that place."
23 So off I went to the valley. Yahweh's glory stood waiting there, as it had by the Chebar canal. I dropped to the ground, face down.
24 The Spirit came inside me and lifted me upright. Then He spoke: "Go home and lock yourself in.
25 Mortal one, they will tie you with ropes, and you will not be able to walk out among the people.
26 I will glue your tongue to the roof of your mouth. Not a word will come out, and you cannot scold them, for they rebel against Me.
27 Later, when I have something to say to you, I will unlock your lips. Then you will announce: 'The Lord Yahweh says so.' Those who choose to hear, will hear. Those who choose to refuse, will refuse. They are rebels."
=a The Hebrew word is shamir, a very hard stone. It may be emery or diamond.
=b With a small change in one Hebrew letter, the text could say, "as the glory of Yahweh rose up from the place where He lives."
=c Tel-abib may mean "hill of new grain." It was probably a Hebrew form of a Babylonian place name.
=d The Hebrew word can also mean "plain."
