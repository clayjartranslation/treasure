# Image credits for Clay Jar Treasure Bible downloads

Each book in the PDF and EPUB opens with a public-domain or CC0 image of its main story, a central figure, or a closely related scene.
The compressed files live in this folder and are used at https://clayjartranslation.github.io/treasure/downloads/images/.
All works below were already in the public domain, or were released under CC0, at the time they were added.
Faithful photographs of two-dimensional public-domain paintings are themselves in the public domain in the United States.
These image files are the same public-domain or CC0 works used in the Clay Jar Translation downloads; the Bible text itself is not CC BY and remains all rights reserved.

When a new book is published, add a fitting Public domain or CC0 image here, record it in this table and in `images/manifest.json`, then rerun `node tools/build-downloads.js`.
