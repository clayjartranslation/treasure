Privacy Policy

This page explains in plain language how this free, ad-free Bible site handles information. It is not legal advice. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Terms of Service Account and backup

The short version

You can read and listen with no account. By default, your place, bookmarks, highlights, listening progress, and settings stay on your device, in this browser. They are not sent to a server.

Signing in is optional. The sign-in screen says what will sync. If you sign in, that is your choice to also save those items to your account so another device can use them. Text size and light or dark stay on the device. There are no ads, no sale of personal information, and no tracking for advertising.

What stays on the device

Until you sign in, and for anything you do not choose to sync, this browser stores:

  • bookmarks and highlights
  • your place in the text
  • listening progress and listening settings, such as speed and voice
  • reading settings such as text size, footnotes, and light or dark

That copy lives in the browser’s local storage. Clearing the site’s data in the browser removes it. A backup file, described below, is a way to keep a copy you control.

Signing in is the opt-in

Nothing in the list above is uploaded because you opened a chapter. Sync starts only after you sign in. The sign-in screen states that signing in will also save, to your account:

  • your place in the text
  • bookmarks
  • highlights
  • where you are in the audio, and listening settings such as speed and voice

The account is the same one the Clay Jar Translation can use. Each Bible keeps its own progress. The sign-in session is stored only in this browser (local persistence). It is not placed in a cookie that other websites can read, and it does not by itself sign you in on another device. Another device syncs only after you sign in there too.

If sign-in is not configured for this copy of the site, the sign-in controls are hidden and nothing is uploaded.

Backup file, with no account

From the Account page, and from Bookmarks and highlights → Backup, you can save a JSON file of bookmarks, highlights, your place, listening progress, and settings. You can import that file later. Import merges it with what is already on the device. It does not require an account, and the file is not uploaded by the site. You choose where to keep it.

Signing out

Signing out asks whether to keep the copy on this device or remove bookmarks, highlights, your place, and listening progress from this device. The account in the cloud is not deleted by signing out. “Delete my data and account” is the separate control that removes the account.

What an account stores

If you sign in, the account is held by Google Firebase. It can include:

  • a user id
  • the email address you used, if you used email or Google
  • a phone number, only if phone sign-in is turned on and you use it (it ships turned off)
  • bookmarks, highlights, reading place, and listening progress and settings, as listed on the sign-in screen

Google sign-in shares the email and name Google provides for that sign-in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. This site does not keep its own copy of your password.

Phone sign-in is built but switched off. Text-message codes cost money and need Firebase’s paid Blaze plan, so they stay off on the free Spark plan. If the owner later turns phone sign-in on, a code is sent by SMS and Google’s invisible reCAPTCHA checks that a person asked for it. Google’s privacy policy and terms describe reCAPTCHA. Until that switch is on, the site does not ask for a phone number.

Who processes data

  • This browser, on your device. The default store for reading data.
  • Google Firebase. Authentication and the account database, only after you sign in. Firebase is a Google service.
  • GitHub Pages. Hosts the website files, including the Bible text. It does not receive your bookmarks.
  • Hugging Face. Hosts the audio recordings the player may request. A request for a chapter’s audio goes to Hugging Face. Your bookmarks are not sent there.

There is no advertising network and no analytics product added by this site to follow you across the web.

Children

The site is a Bible reader for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect account information from a child under 13. In the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and Switzerland, where the age of digital consent is 16, it does not knowingly collect account information from anyone under 16 without a parent or guardian’s consent. Reading without an account does not send account data to us. If you are a parent and believe an account was created for a child, write to the contact address below and ask for it to be deleted.

Lawful basis (GDPR and UK GDPR)

If you only read on the device, this site is not running an account about you. If you sign in, the account data is processed to provide the save-across-devices feature you asked for. That is a contract with you for that feature. You can refuse by not signing in, and you can stop by deleting the account. Where a local law needs a different basis, the basis is the one that law requires for an optional feature you turned on.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the account data, and you can object or ask for a restriction. You can also complain to a privacy regulator. Practical ways on this site:

  • A copy you can carry. Save the backup file. That is a portable JSON copy of bookmarks, highlights, place, listening progress, and settings.
  • Erasure. Use Delete my data and account. That removes the sign-in and the saved progress for both Bibles in the account, and the copy of that progress on this device.
  • Access or a question. Write to [contact email]. The owner replaces that placeholder with a real address. Do not send secrets to the placeholder.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

This site does not sell personal information. It does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. There are no ads. If you sign in, the account data is used to keep your place and notes, not to advertise. You may ask for access or deletion using the contact address and the delete control above.

Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, the optional account is processed to provide the feature you requested. You may ask for confirmation, access, correction, and deletion, and you may petition the national authority (ANPD). Use the backup file, Delete my data and account, or the contact address.

Canada (PIPEDA)

Account data is limited to what the optional sync needs. You can ask for access or a correction through the contact address. You can delete the account yourself. The device copy remains under your control when you are signed out.

Australia (Privacy Act)

If you sign in, account data may be handled by Google Firebase outside Australia, and the public site files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Audio files may be requested from Hugging Face. Those companies describe their own handling of overseas storage. You can ask for access or correction through the contact address, and you can delete the account on the Account page.

International transfers

Firebase, GitHub Pages, and Hugging Face may process data in the United States and in other countries. Their own terms describe how they handle those transfers. This site does not run a separate server for bookmarks. If you do not sign in, reading data is not transferred to those account systems.

How long data is kept

The copy on the device stays until you clear it, sign out and choose to remove it, or delete the account (which also clears that progress on this device). Account data in Firebase stays until you delete the account. The Bible text and audio files stay published as part of the site. This site does not keep its own server logs of what you read.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the page and the date at the top are updated. The new text applies as you keep using the site. If you have an account, it applies as you keep using sign-in. A change does not turn on sync by itself. Sync still starts only when you sign in.

Contact
